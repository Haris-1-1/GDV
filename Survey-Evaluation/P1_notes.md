## Chart 1: Motivation Level (boxplot)

**Q1. What does this chart show?**

> "Three groups. Low, medium, high motivation, and how they relate to exam score. Honestly they look really similar to each other."

Observation: noticed the visual similarity of the three groups almost immediately.

**Q2. What is unclear or confusing?**

> "The dots above and below the boxes. I had to think for a second to realize those are outliers."

**Q3. What would you change?**

> "Maybe a short note explaining what each part of the box means. And the title could already tell me the takeaway."

## Chart 2: Family Income (bar chart)

**Q1. What does this chart show?**

> "Average exam score per income group. It goes up from low to high."

**Q2. What is unclear or confusing?**

> "Nothing really, the chart is clear. But the bars look more different from each other than they actually are. I think the y-axis doesn't start at zero."

**Q3. What would you change?**

> "Start the y-axis at zero. And maybe somewhere mention that the difference is actually small, otherwise people will think income matters a lot."

---

## Chart 3: Attendance vs Exam Score (scatterplot)

**Q1. What does this chart show?**

> "How often someone shows up versus what they score on the exam. The points go up, more attendance means higher scores."

**Q2. What is unclear or confusing?**

> "There are so many points stacked on top of each other in the middle. I can't really tell how many students are in that area."

**Q3. What would you change?**

> "Make the dots smaller and more see-through. And I would add a trend line so the direction is obvious immediately."

## Closing

**Q4. Which chart was the easiest to understand?**

> "The bar chart, family income. Bars are just easy."

**Q5. Which chart was the most difficult?**
