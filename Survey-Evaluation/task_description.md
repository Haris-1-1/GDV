# Task Description

## Goal

The goal of the evaluation was to check whether the three main visualizations are understandable for a viewer who is not familiar with the dataset, and to identify any parts that are confusing or could be improved before submission.

The three charts evaluated were:

1. **Chart 1**: Attendance vs Exam Score (scatterplot)
2. **Chart 2**: Average Exam Score by Family Income (bar chart)
3. **Chart 3**: Exam Score by Motivation Level (boxplot)

## Method

The evaluation used a structured qualitative interview. Each participant was shown the three charts one by one and answered a fixed set of open questions about what the chart shows, what is unclear, and what they would change. 

Each session took roughly 15 minutes.

## Participants

Four participants took part. They covered a small range of visualization literacy, from people familiar with charts in an academic context to non-technical readers.

| ID | Background |
|---|---|
| P1 | Fellow student |
| P2 | Fellow student |
| P3 | Family member |
| P4 | Friend |

Real names are not used.

## Setup

- Charts were shown as static images on a laptop screen.
- Sessions were conducted in person.
- Notes were made during each session and typed up immediately afterwards.

## Outputs

- `P1_notes.md` to `P4_notes.md`: anonymised notes per participant.
- The synthesis of these notes and the design changes that followed are documented in Sections 4 and 5 of the project report.
