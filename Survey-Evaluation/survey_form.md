# Survey Form

The same three questions were asked to each participant for each of the three charts. Two closing questions were asked once at the end of the session.

## Per chart (asked three times, once for each chart)

1. **What does this chart show?**
2. **What is unclear or confusing?**
3. **What would you change?**

## Closing questions (asked once at the end)

4. **Which chart was the easiest to understand?**
5. **Which chart was the most difficult to understand?**

## Notes for the moderator

- Ask the questions in this exact order.
- Let the participant answer fully before moving on. If they hesitate, give them a moment instead of jumping in.
- Do not lead the participant toward a specific answer. If they ask a question back, redirect them gently ("just say what you see").
- Try to capture the exact phrasing the participant uses, not a paraphrase.
